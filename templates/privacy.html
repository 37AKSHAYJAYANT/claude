Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use Spendly, we collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, password hash, and the financial transaction data you log (such as expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions).

We do not collect or store your banking passwords, credit card numbers, or bank account credentials. All expense entries are manually logged or imported by you.

2. Use of Service & Data Processing

Spendly processes your personal and expense data solely to provide, maintain, and enhance the tracking tools available to you. This includes computing category totals, generating spending breakdowns, and calculating monthly summaries.

We will never sell, rent, or monetize your individual financial information or personal data to third parties or advertisers.

3. User Data Ownership & Control

You retain full ownership of your data. You may view, edit, or delete your expense records at any time through the application interface.

Upon account deletion, all associated personal profiles and expense logs will be permanently deleted from our primary databases in accordance with standard retention schedules.

4. Privacy Warnings & User Responsibility

Account Confidentiality: You are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure. Avoid sharing your password with others or logging into Spendly on untrusted public networks and shared devices without signing out.

Sensitive Notes: We strongly advise against entering highly confidential identifiers (such as complete credit card numbers, bank PINs, or national identity numbers) in the optional expense note or description fields.

5. Limitations of Liability

While we employ standard security best practices to safeguard your data, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely impenetrable.

Spendly and its developers disclaim liability for unauthorized access, data loss, or disclosure resulting from cyber attacks, user negligence, or events beyond our reasonable control, to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in service functionality or legal requirements. Material changes will be highlighted with an updated date at the top of this policy.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at privacy@spendly.app.
